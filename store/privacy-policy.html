Privacy Policy

Bluetooth Terminal for Android · Effective 14 October 2026

Bluetooth Terminal ("the app") is developed by Majid Bahmani. This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: it collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not have permission to access the internet, so it cannot send any data off your phone.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth to find Bluetooth Low Energy devices near you, connect to the one you choose, and exchange data with it. For this it asks for two Android permissions:

  • Nearby devices – scan (BLUETOOTH_SCAN): to list devices around you. The app declares that it never uses scan results to determine your location.
  • Nearby devices – connect (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT): to connect to a device and send and receive data.

Device names, addresses, signal strength and the messages you send and receive are only shown on your screen while the app runs. They are not saved after you close the app and are never shared.

Location

The app does not ask for location permission and does not use your location.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
