3. Permissions the app asks for and why

  • Location (Android): required by the OS to scan for Bluetooth and Wi-Fi devices. We use it only to discover devices nearby; we do not record or transmit your location.
  • Bluetooth: to discover and pair devices over BLE.
  • Wi-Fi / local network / multicast: to find and talk to devices on your local network.
  • Microphone: only after you tap the microphone button in the AI designer chat, to turn your speech into text. See section 4 for how audio is handled.
  • Storage / files: to import or export files you choose.
  • Camera (if enabled): to scan QR codes for pairing or sharing, and to show camera elements you add to a device screen. Camera images are processed on your device and are not sent to us.

You can revoke any permission in your OS settings; the related feature will stop working.

4. Voice input

Voice input works only while you have turned it on with the microphone button. You choose how speech is turned into text (long-press the microphone button):

  • Vosk + Gemini (default): speech is recognized on your device by the Vosk speech engine. When a sentence is unclear, the audio of that sentence and the on-device draft are sent to Google Gemini for transcription.
  • Gemini only: you tap to start and tap to stop recording; the recording is sent to Google Gemini for transcription.
  • Vosk only: speech is recognized on your device only; no audio leaves your device.
  • OS SR: your phone's own speech recognizer (usually provided by Google) handles the audio, on your device when the phone supports it, otherwise under that provider's privacy policy. The app receives text only.

Audio sent to Google Gemini goes directly from your device to Google using the Gemini API key you entered, and is processed under Google's terms and privacy policy. We do not receive, store or keep copies of your recordings. The on-device Vosk speech model is downloaded once from alphacephei.com, the Vosk project's website; no personal data is sent with that download.

5. Third parties we use

  • Amazon Web Services (Cognito, AppSync, DynamoDB, IoT Core, Lambda, S3) — authentication, data storage, device messaging. Data is stored in the AWS region eu-north-1 (Stockholm, Sweden).
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in method.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — the usage statistics in section 2, processed by Google on our behalf. They are not linked to your email address.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase — payments. We never see your card details.
  • Google Gemini / OpenAI — only if you add your own API key. Your prompts, and the voice recordings and transcribed speech described in section 4, are sent directly to the provider you chose, under that provider's privacy policy. Your key is stored only on your device.
  • alphacephei.com — download of the on-device Vosk speech model (section 4).

We do not sell your data.

6. Retention

  • Account data: kept while your account is active and deleted within 30 days after you delete your account.
  • Message metadata and billing records: kept for up to 7 years for billing, dispute and legal (tax) purposes.
  • Diagnostics: 90 days.
  • Usage statistics: deleted automatically after at most 14 months.
  • Voice recordings: not kept by us (section 4).

7. Deleting your account and data

To delete your account, email majidbahmaniapp@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, using the subject "Delete my CyLinko account". You don't need the app installed. We confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days. The steps are also on the account deletion page.

Deleting your account removes your account, devices, channels, device definitions, templates, certificates, sharing records and uploaded files (such as your profile photo), and forfeits any remaining CyCoin. Billing records may be kept as described above where the law requires.

8. Children

CyLinko is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Portugal, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados, CNPD). Contact majidbahmaniapp@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

10. Security

Data in transit is encrypted (TLS / mutual-TLS for devices). Access to our backend is authenticated per user. No system is perfectly secure; keep your credentials safe and tell us at majidbahmaniapp@gmail.com if you suspect a breach.

11. Changes

We will notify you in the app of material changes and update the version and effective date at the top of this page.
