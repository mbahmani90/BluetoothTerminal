Delete your CyLinko account

CyLinko for Android and iOS · by Majid Bahmani · Updated 18 November 2026

You can ask us to delete your CyLinko account and the data linked to it at any time. You don't need the app installed to do this.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to majidbahmaniapp@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to CyLinko.
  2. Use the subject Delete my CyLinko account. You don't need to give a reason.
  3. We reply to confirm that the request came from the account's owner, then delete the account within 30 days and let you know when it's done.

What we delete

  • Your account: email address, name, Google sign-in link and account user id.
  • Your channels, devices, device definitions, GUI templates, certificates and device sharing records.
  • Files you uploaded, such as your profile photo and channel icons.
  • Your remaining CyCoin balance, which is forfeited and can't be refunded.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Billing records (purchase receipts, CyCoin transactions and message metadata used for billing): kept for up to 7 years where tax and accounting law requires it, then deleted.
  • Usage statistics in Google Analytics for Firebase: not linked to your email address, and deleted automatically after the analytics retention period (at most 14 months).

Devices you own keep any data stored on the device itself. To remove it, reset the device.
